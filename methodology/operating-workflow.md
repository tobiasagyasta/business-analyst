# Operating Workflow

Apply `methodology/indonesia-and-currency.md` throughout this workflow.

## Standard Path

1. **Decision intake:** create `decisions/<decision-id>/brief.md`; record status as `proposed`.
2. **Context assembly:** reference only relevant shared and business context, then identify missing critical evidence.
3. **Routing and plan:** use `agents/registry.md` to select capabilities and write precise specialist assignments. Choose light or full path based on reversibility and materiality.
4. **Specialist analysis:** run independent assignments in parallel; store outputs in `analyses/`.
5. **Synthesis:** the Business Strategist reconciles evidence, scope, and disagreements.
6. **Challenge and options:** challenge assumptions, then compare viable options before a proposed recommendation.
7. **Human decision:** record approval, rejection, deferral, or experimental authorization in the Decision Record. No execution is implied by a proposal.
8. **Execution planning:** after approval or experiment authorization, request the necessary Operations, Sales, and Marketing handoffs.
9. **Measurement and review:** measure against predefined criteria; populate actual outcome and lessons, then update durable context only with reusable, sourced knowledge.

If specialist findings conflict, preserve both claims and sources, identify why they differ (scope, date, method, or assumption), and request targeted follow-up only if it could change the decision.

## Routing Examples

### A. Price Change

For "Should coffee prices increase?", route Pricing Analyst, FP&A Analyst, and Business Strategist. Add Trend Researcher only if current competitor or customer-price evidence is missing. Do not select Sales or Marketing unless the decision includes offer communication or acquisition effects. A low-risk price test can follow the light path; a broad permanent increase needs options and challenge review.

### B. Customer Acquisition

For "I want more customers for the breakfast kiosk next month", route Marketing Growth Hacker, Offer & Lead Gen Strategist, and Product Trend Researcher when segment or local demand evidence is needed. Finance is unnecessary unless spend or economics are material; Operations is unnecessary unless capacity or service delivery is likely constrained. Prefer a time-bounded experiment with clear metrics.

### C. New Location / Major Investment

For "Should we open another coffee-shop location?", route Business Strategist, FP&A Analyst, Operations Manager, Product Trend Researcher, and Pricing Analyst when local offer economics matter. Require cash and scenario analysis, operating capacity and supplier feasibility, demand/competition research, explicit options including defer, challenge review, decision gates, and human approval before commitment.

## Context Efficiency

Each assignment receives: applicable methodology, targeted shared context, targeted business context, the Decision Brief, and specialist-specific evidence. Agents may inspect another file only when it materially affects their task; they should cite it rather than reproduce it.

Use `methodology/financial-and-recommendation-standards.md` whenever a decision contains financial reasoning or a proposed recommendation.
