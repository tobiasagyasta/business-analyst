# Evidence and Confidence

Apply `methodology/indonesia-and-currency.md` to market scope and monetary evidence.

## Claim Classification

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `FACT` | Directly supported by a reliable source or measurement. |
| `OBSERVATION` | A recorded pattern or report; it may not explain why it occurred. |
| `INFERENCE` | A reasoned interpretation of facts or observations. |
| `HYPOTHESIS` | A testable explanation or prediction not yet validated. |
| `ASSUMPTION` | An input accepted temporarily for analysis; it is not evidence. |
| `UNKNOWN` | Needed information is unavailable or insufficient. |
| `CONFLICTING EVIDENCE` | Credible sources disagree or point in different directions. |

Material claims should identify: classification, source, date/freshness, business and location scope, confidence, and limitation. Do not relabel an assumption as a fact because it appears in a calculation.

## Confidence

Confidence measures evidence quality and applicability, not how persuasive the prose sounds.

- `HIGH`: multiple reliable, recent, scope-matched sources; important assumptions are tested or low impact.
- `MEDIUM`: useful but incomplete, older, indirect, or partially scope-matched evidence; outcomes remain sensitive to assumptions.
- `LOW`: sparse, weak, unverified, conflicting, or non-transferable evidence; material inputs are unknown.

State what would raise confidence. For a low-confidence, reversible decision, prefer a bounded experiment. For a low-confidence, costly or hard-to-reverse decision, defer commitment or collect critical evidence first.
