# Options and Challenge Review

Apply `methodology/indonesia-and-currency.md`; compare monetary options in `IDR` / `Rp`.

## Options

Important decisions must present meaningful alternatives, including `do nothing` or `defer` when it is viable. The number of options should fit the decision, not a fixed quota.

Compare each option where relevant: revenue, margin, cash requirement, operational difficulty, strategic alignment, customer risk, reversibility, time to result, downside risk, and confidence. Identify decision gates and what is deliberately deprioritized.

## Challenge Review

Before finalizing a material recommendation, the strategist explicitly reviews:

- Which assumptions the recommendation depends on and which have weak evidence.
- What would make it wrong, including lower demand or competitor response.
- Correlation mistaken for causation or over-extrapolation of recent results.
- Fixed versus variable cost errors, opportunity costs, and profit versus cash-flow differences.
- Operational bottlenecks, capacity limits, and portfolio trade-offs.
- Whether a cheaper experiment could reduce uncertainty first.

Record challenges that change an option, confidence, or recommendation. This contract can later be assigned to a dedicated `specialized/strategy-critic` without changing the workflow.
