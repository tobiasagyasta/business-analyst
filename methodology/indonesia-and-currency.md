# Indonesia Market and Currency Standard

This repository supports businesses operating in the Indonesian market unless a Decision Brief explicitly defines a different scope. Analyze local customer behavior, competition, channels, suppliers, operating conditions, regulations, taxes, labor, and geography only to the extent supported by relevant evidence.

## Currency

- Use Indonesian Rupiah (`IDR` / `Rp`) for every internal monetary value, target, budget, price, cost, forecast, and calculation.
- State the amount and period or unit, for example `Rp 25,000 per drink` or `Rp 15,000,000 per month`.
- Do not use `$`, `€`, or other currencies for internal business context or recommendations.
- Preserve an external source's original currency only when necessary. Show the source currency, exchange-rate source and date, conversion method, and resulting `IDR` amount. If these are unavailable, mark the IDR equivalent `UNKNOWN`.

## Evidence and Scope

- Label the Indonesian scope precisely: national, province, city/regency, neighborhood, delivery radius, or a named location.
- Do not generalize national evidence to a local decision without noting the limitation.
- Identify Indonesian regulatory, tax, licensing, halal, labor, platform, and local-market implications only when applicable and supported by current evidence. Mark them `UNKNOWN` when unverified.
